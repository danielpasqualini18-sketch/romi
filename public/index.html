Weight tracker

The 7-day trend averages all weigh-ins within each local calendar day, then fits a straight line through the daily averages over seven dates ending at the latest weigh-in. Each recorded day has equal weight; missing days are left empty. Individual readings and scale colours remain visible. Mixing scales, clothing and feeding times can still affect the result.
