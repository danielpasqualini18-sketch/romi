Weight tracker

The 7-day average uses the first and latest weigh-ins within the week ending at the latest measurement, divided by their actual gap. The number of covered days is shown. Calendar days in the configured timezone are used when exact times are unrecorded. Different scales, clothes and timing can change readings. This is an observed trend, not an assessment of growth.
